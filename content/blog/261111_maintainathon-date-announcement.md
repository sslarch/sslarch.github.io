As mentioned in our [previous blogpost](https://sslarch.github.io/blog/gearing-up-for-2027-maintainathon/) about the upcoming [Digital Archaeology Maintainathon](https://sslarch.github.io/maintainathon/), we finally settled on a date. The next Digital Archaeology Maintainathon will take place on **18-22 January 2027**, mark the date!  

For the occasion, Zack Batiste redesigned the website, including a list of resources of easier access. If you think something is missing, or would like to get involved in any way, reach out to us and we will gladly help you add more content and give you credit on the [resources webpage](https://sslarch.github.io/maintainathon/resources.html). Please let us know on Matrix or [Mastodon](https://archaeo.social/@CAA_SSLA)!

We are also welcoming anyone to join us in the maintainathon, even if you don't have links with computational archaeology. The initiative is ultimately about visibility for these often forgotten parts of our profession. We will use the #DigiArchMaintainathon hashtag, but we welcome any hashtag and input that you can come up with and tie them all together. Again, we want to create a space for collaboration. 

We hope to hear from your social medias in January!
